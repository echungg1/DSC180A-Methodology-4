The most interesting topic we covered in my domain is the differences between how childern learn language versus how language models learn language. By learning more about the BabyLM challenge and reading past submission papers, I've realized how different humans are from LLM's (in terms of language acquisition) and am excited to continue to explore how we can alter the model or input to better improve language models on limited data.

Children are more receptive to motherese (simple sentences spoken at higher tones with a lot of inflections) than other forms of input. It would be interesting to test this on language models.
